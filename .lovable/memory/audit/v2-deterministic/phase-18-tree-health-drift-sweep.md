## Phase 18 lessons

1. **Drift sweeps are NOT no-ops** even at 168/168 tree-health — sister gates
   (trace-map, freshness, folder-refs) can drift independently of tree-health
   and need independent verification. Run all 5 gates in a drift sweep, not
   just the named one.
2. **Rebaseline-vs-bind decision** has a clear test: if `ac_traced` flat and
   `code_orphan` flat, drift growth = AC growth = rebaseline. If `ac_traced`
   dropped or `code_orphan` grew, real regression = bind/fix required.
3. The `--update-baseline` flag is a 1-second escape hatch — but the discipline
   is the 50-AC inspection threshold, not the flag itself.
